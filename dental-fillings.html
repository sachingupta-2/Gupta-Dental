IN SIMPLE WORDS

What is restorative dentistry?

Teeth can become damaged for many reasons. Decay, cracks, injuries, old fillings or years of wear can change the way a tooth looks and functions.

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing these teeth and helping them work properly again.

The aim is simple: preserve as much of your natural tooth as possible and restore its function.

🦷

Not every damaged tooth needs to be removed.

Depending on the condition of the tooth, there may be several ways to repair and protect it.

HOW CAN WE HELP?
