ABOUT DR. GUPTA

Dr. Tanupriya Gupta

Principal Dentist

Dr. Tanupriya Gupta is a dedicated dental professional with a strong academic and clinical background spanning more than two decades. Her approach to dentistry combines extensive experience with a simple philosophy — to preserve natural teeth whenever they can be predictably saved and maintained.

She graduated from Nair Hospital Dental College, Mumbai, in 2003, and began her professional journey as a House Surgeon at PGI, Chandigarh, gaining valuable experience in a busy clinical environment.

She subsequently pursued her postgraduate education at Manipal College of Dental Sciences, Manipal. Following her postgraduation, she worked at AIIMS, New Delhi, as a Senior Resident for three years and subsequently as a Senior Research Fellow for one year.

She subsequently joined the Faculty of Dental Sciences, Jamia Millia Islamia, combining clinical practice with teaching and academic work.

In 2013, she established her own dental clinic in South Delhi, creating a practice centred around personalised care, clinical precision and long-term oral health.

Preserving Natural Teeth With Precision

Dr. Gupta is particularly passionate about saving natural teeth whenever possible, with a special interest in long-lasting restorative treatment and root canal therapy.

To support this philosophy, the clinic combines clinical experience with modern magnification and dental technology. Treatment is carried out using high-magnification dental loupes with 7.5× magnification, helping improve visualisation and precision during detailed procedures.

The clinic also incorporates modern technology for endodontic treatment (ECT) and follows stringent sterilisation and infection-control protocols, with appropriate equipment and processes designed to maintain a high standard of patient safety.
