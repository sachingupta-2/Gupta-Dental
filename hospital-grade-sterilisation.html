Our Difference

Hospital-Grade Sterilisation

Every instrument, every visit, without exception — a stringent sterilisation protocol that's never treated as optional.

← Back to Our Difference

Why this isn't just a checkbox

Cross-contamination in a dental setting is entirely preventable — but only if sterilisation is treated as a strict, non-negotiable protocol rather than a formality. At Dr. Gupta's Dental Clinic, every single instrument that touches a patient is sterilised before that visit, with no exceptions made for time pressure or a busy day.

The protocol, step by step

  1. 1. Pre-cleaning Instruments are rinsed and scrubbed immediately after use to remove any organic debris before sterilisation.
  2. 2. Ultrasonic cleaning An ultrasonic bath removes residue from hard-to-reach areas that manual cleaning alone can miss.
  3. 3. Sealed pouching Each instrument is individually sealed in a sterilisation pouch, keeping it protected until the moment it's opened for your treatment.
  4. 4. Autoclave sterilisation Pouches go through a high-pressure steam autoclave cycle — the same standard used in hospital operating rooms.
  5. 5. Cycle verification Every autoclave cycle is checked and logged, so nothing reaches a patient without a confirmed complete cycle.
  6. 6. Opened chairside, in front of you Your sealed instrument pouch is opened in the treatment room, at the start of your visit — not before.

What to look for, and what we do

Corners sometimes cut

  • Instruments reused across patients same day
  • No visible sealed pouches at chairside
  • Sterilisation treated as background process

Our protocol
