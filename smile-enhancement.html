A PERSONAL APPROACH

Your smile should look like you.

Smile enhancement is not about making every smile look the same. It is about understanding what you would like to improve and considering what is appropriate for your teeth, facial features and overall oral health.

Depending on the individual situation, treatment may involve one or more conservative aesthetic procedures. The focus is on careful planning, proportion and maintaining a natural appearance.

✦

Enhancement, not exaggeration.

We believe aesthetic dentistry should complement your natural smile rather than make it look artificial.

WHAT CAN BE CONSIDERED?

Small changes can make a meaningful difference.

Every smile is different. During consultation, we assess the areas you would like to improve and discuss which options may be suitable.

POSSIBLE OPTIONS

Treatment is selected according to your needs.

Not every smile requires the same treatment. The appropriate option depends on your teeth, existing restorations, oral health and aesthetic goals.

01

Professional Tooth Whitening

Where appropriate, professional whitening may be considered to improve the shade of natural teeth.

02

Composite Bonding

Tooth-coloured restorative material may be used in selected cases to modify the shape, contour or appearance of a tooth.

03

Aesthetic Restorations

Existing or damaged teeth may sometimes be restored using carefully selected tooth-coloured restorative materials.

04

Other Aesthetic Solutions

Depending on the clinical situation, other cosmetic or restorative options may be discussed during your consultation.
