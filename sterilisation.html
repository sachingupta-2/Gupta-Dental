Clean is not the same as sterile.

Instruments may look clean, but microscopic germs can still be present. Sterilisation is a validated process that eliminates all forms of microbial life, including highly resistant bacterial spores.

♢   Your safety is our priority.

We use internationally accepted infection-control protocols and modern sterilisation equipment to keep our patients and team safe.

What is an autoclave?

An autoclave is a specialised sterilisation machine that uses pressurised steam at high temperature to sterilise suitable reusable dental instruments. It works on the same basic principle as a pressure cooker, but is designed for medical and dental use, with carefully controlled cycles.

Think of it like a pressure cooker.

Just as a pressure cooker uses heat and pressure to cook food faster, an autoclave uses pressurised steam to sterilise instruments at high temperature.
