REPLACING MISSING TEETH

One missing tooth or several, the approach is individual.

Tooth replacement is not a one-size-fits-all treatment. The right solution depends on the number and position of missing teeth, the condition of the surrounding teeth and gums, your bite, your preferences and your overall oral health.

01

Restore Function

Replacing missing teeth can help restore comfortable chewing and support everyday oral function.

02

Support Your Smile

A well-planned replacement can help restore the appearance of your smile while complementing your remaining teeth.

03

Protect What Remains

Appropriate tooth replacement can form part of a broader plan to maintain oral function and the health of your remaining teeth.

YOUR OPTIONS

Different ways to replace missing teeth.

Depending on your individual situation, there may be several ways to replace a missing tooth or multiple teeth. We discuss the advantages, limitations and suitability of each option before treatment.

01

Dental Implants

Dental implants can provide a stable foundation for a replacement tooth or other implant-supported restoration. Suitability depends on factors such as oral health, bone availability and the planned restoration.

Explore Dental Implants →
02

Fixed Dental Bridges

A dental bridge can replace one or more missing teeth by using the neighbouring teeth or suitable supporting structures to hold the replacement in position.

Discuss Bridge Options →
03

Removable Dentures

Removable partial or complete dentures can provide a practical option for replacing multiple or all missing teeth, depending on the individual case.

Discuss Denture Options →
04

Implant-Supported Solutions

In selected cases, implants may be used to support certain types of bridges or dentures, providing an alternative approach to conventional removable replacements.

CAREFUL PLANNING

The replacement should fit your mouth, not just the gap.

Replacing a missing tooth is about more than filling an empty space. We consider your existing teeth, gums, bite, available support and the way your teeth work together.

This allows us to discuss treatment options that are appropriate for your individual circumstances rather than recommending the same solution for everyone.

YOUR TREATMENT, YOUR NEEDS

There is no single answer for every missing tooth.

The best replacement option depends on your specific circumstances. During your consultation, we can explain the available choices and help you understand what each option involves.

01

One Missing Tooth

Depending on the surrounding teeth and supporting tissues, options may include an implant-supported crown or a fixed bridge.

02

Several Missing Teeth

Multiple missing teeth may be replaced using bridges, implants, removable dentures or combinations of treatment options.

03

Complete Tooth Loss

Where all teeth are missing, complete dentures or selected implant-supported solutions may be considered.

04

Existing Dental Work

Existing crowns, bridges, fillings and other dental work are considered when developing an overall treatment plan.
