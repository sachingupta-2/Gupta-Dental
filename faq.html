Before your visit

We believe patients should understand their dental care before treatment begins.

If your question isn't answered here, we're always happy to discuss it during your consultation.

Your first visit generally begins with a conversation about your concerns, dental history and what you would like help with. This is followed by a careful clinical examination to understand your oral health and identify any areas that may need attention.

The findings are discussed with you before any treatment is recommended, so you can understand the available options and make an informed decision.

Not all dental problems cause pain in their early stages. Regular examinations can help identify changes before they become more complicated or uncomfortable.

The appropriate frequency of visits depends on your individual dental health and risk factors.

There is no single schedule that is suitable for everyone. Your recommended review interval depends on your oral health, previous dental problems, existing restorations and individual risk factors.

Your dentist can suggest an appropriate follow-up schedule after assessing your dental health.

You can contact the clinic directly to discuss an appointment. Our team can help you find a suitable time and guide you regarding your visit.

Root canal treatment is performed with appropriate local anaesthesia to keep you comfortable during the procedure.

Some sensitivity or discomfort can occur after treatment, but this varies between patients and depends on the condition of the tooth.

A filling may be recommended when tooth structure has been damaged by decay or another localised defect.

The appropriate treatment depends on the size, depth and condition of the affected tooth. The aim is to address the problem while preserving healthy tooth structure wherever possible.

Even with regular brushing, plaque and hardened deposits can accumulate in areas that are difficult to clean effectively at home.

Professional cleaning can help remove these deposits and is often combined with guidance on improving everyday oral hygiene.

Where clinically appropriate, preserving healthy natural tooth structure is an important part of our approach.

Treatment is planned after careful examination and diagnosis, with the goal of addressing the problem while avoiding unnecessary removal of healthy tooth structure.

Children benefit from dental care that focuses not only on treating problems but also on developing healthy habits and preventing future dental issues.

The appropriate timing of the first visit can depend on your child's age, dental development and individual needs.

A calm, age-appropriate approach can help children become more comfortable with dental visits. The aim is to create a positive experience and gradually build familiarity with the dental environment.

Sudden dental pain can have different causes, so an examination is important to understand what is happening.

If you experience significant pain, swelling, trauma, a broken tooth or another urgent dental problem, contact the clinic so that your situation can be assessed and the appropriate next step discussed.

Contact the clinic as soon as possible for advice and assessment. The appropriate treatment depends on how the tooth has been damaged and whether the surrounding tissues are also affected.
