Unhurried Appointments

Why rushing costs more than time

A packed schedule of ten-minute slots leaves no room to explain what's actually happening in your mouth, answer your questions properly, or double-check work before you're moved out of the chair. Dr. Gupta's Dental Clinic deliberately schedules fewer patients per day than a high-turnover practice would — so every appointment has the time it actually needs, not the time a calendar allows for.
