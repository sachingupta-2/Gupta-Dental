What is a rubber dam?

A rubber dam is a thin, flexible sheet made of latex or non-latex material that is placed around the tooth being treated. It isolates the tooth from the rest of the mouth, creating a clean, dry and controlled working environment.

♡

Think of it like masking tape when painting a wall.

When you paint a wall, you use masking tape to keep the paint only on the area you want. A rubber dam works in a similar way — it keeps the treatment area separate from saliva, tongue and cheeks so we can work more accurately.

Why do we use a rubber dam?

During many dental procedures, especially root canal treatment, keeping the tooth isolated helps us work more precisely. It also reduces contamination from saliva and provides an additional safety measure by helping prevent small instruments or materials from being swallowed or inhaled.

How does it look?

A rubber dam is a small sheet that fits around the tooth with the help of a small clamp. It may look different from what you are used to, but it is simple, comfortable and very helpful during treatment.
