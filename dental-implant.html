DENTAL IMPLANTS

Replacing missing teeth with confidence.

Dental implants can provide a stable, functional and natural-looking way to replace missing teeth. Treatment begins with careful assessment and planning to determine whether an implant-supported restoration is appropriate for you.

WHY DENTAL IMPLANTS

A considered solution for missing teeth.

Losing a tooth can affect more than appearance. It can influence chewing, comfort and the way the remaining teeth function together. Dental implants are one option for replacing missing teeth and supporting a restoration designed around your individual needs.

01

Stability

An implant-supported restoration is designed to provide a stable replacement for a missing tooth.

02

Function

Replacing a missing tooth can help restore comfortable chewing and support everyday oral function.

03

Natural Appearance

The final restoration is planned to complement the surrounding teeth and your overall smile.

UNDERSTANDING IMPLANTS

More than replacing what is visible.

A dental implant is a small fixture placed within the jawbone to support a replacement tooth. Once the implant has integrated with the surrounding bone, it can support a crown or another suitable restoration.

The treatment is not simply about placing an implant. Careful examination, planning, implant placement and restoration all form part of the process.

✓
Individual assessment

Your dental and oral health is assessed before treatment is planned.

✓
Detailed treatment planning

The proposed restoration and implant position are considered together.

✓
Long-term maintenance

Good oral hygiene and regular reviews remain important after treatment.

OUR APPROACH

Thoughtful planning, step by step.

Every implant case is different. Our approach begins with understanding your oral health and ends with a restoration designed to work comfortably within your smile.

01

Assessment

We examine your teeth, gums, bite and overall oral condition to understand your individual situation.

02

Planning

Your treatment options are discussed and the implant position and final restoration are carefully planned.

03

Implant Placement

The implant is placed using a carefully planned clinical approach appropriate to your individual case.

04

Restoration

Once healing and integration are appropriate, the planned restoration is fitted and adjusted for function and appearance.

THE BENEFITS

Designed around function and comfort.

When appropriately planned and maintained, implant treatment can provide a dependable option for replacing missing teeth.

01

Replace a missing tooth

A single missing tooth can be replaced with an implant-supported crown where appropriate.

02

Support a larger restoration

Implants may also be used to support certain multiple-tooth or other restorative solutions.

03

Restore everyday function

A properly planned restoration can help improve chewing comfort and oral function.

04

Maintain a natural-looking smile

The restoration is designed with consideration for the surrounding teeth and smile.

IS IMPLANT TREATMENT RIGHT FOR YOU?

The right treatment starts with assessment.

Dental implants are not automatically the right solution for every missing tooth. Your suitability depends on factors such as your oral health, available bone, gum condition, general health and the type of restoration required.

01

A single missing tooth

An implant-supported crown may be considered when a single tooth needs to be replaced.

02

Multiple missing teeth

Implant-supported solutions may be considered for replacing several missing teeth.

03

Patients seeking a fixed option

Implants can be considered when a stable, implant-supported replacement is desired and clinically appropriate.

04

Patients needing individual planning

Every case requires an assessment to determine the most appropriate treatment options.

AFTER YOUR IMPLANT

Treatment doesn't end when the tooth is placed.

Maintaining your implant and surrounding tissues is an important part of long-term care. Good oral hygiene, regular professional care and periodic clinical review help monitor the health and function of the restoration.

✓

Maintain careful daily oral hygiene.

✓

Attend recommended dental reviews.

✓

Professional cleaning when required.

✓

Report changes in comfort or function promptly.
