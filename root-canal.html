What is root canal treatment?

Root canal treatment is a procedure used to treat infection or inflammation inside a tooth. The infected or damaged pulp is carefully removed, the internal space is cleaned and disinfected, and the tooth is sealed to help prevent further infection.

The aim is to preserve your natural tooth and restore its function instead of removing it whenever the tooth can be successfully saved.

♡

Your natural tooth deserves careful treatment.

With proper diagnosis, planning and restoration, a treated tooth can continue to support your bite and everyday oral function.

When might a root canal be needed?

Root canal treatment may be recommended when the pulp inside a tooth becomes inflamed, infected or irreversibly damaged.

A thorough examination and appropriate diagnostic tests help us understand the condition of the tooth and select the right treatment.

◯

Persistent or spontaneous tooth pain

♢

Prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold

⌖

Pain while chewing or biting

+

Swelling or tenderness near the tooth

◌

Deep decay or damage to the tooth

How is root canal treatment performed?

Each case is assessed individually. The treatment process may vary depending on the condition of the tooth, the extent of infection and the restoration required afterward.

01

Removal of damaged pulp

We carefully remove the damaged or infected pulp from inside the tooth before cleaning, shaping and preparing it for a proper root canal treatment.

02

Cleaning the canal

The damaged pulp is removed and the internal canal system is carefully cleaned and disinfected.

03

Sealing the tooth

The cleaned canal space is filled and sealed to help reduce the possibility of reinfection.

04

Final restoration

The tooth is restored according to its condition and functional requirements to help protect it for the future.

Our approach to root canal treatment

Successful treatment begins with careful planning and attention to detail. We focus on preserving healthy tooth structure and providing treatment based on your individual needs.

01

Careful diagnosis

Understanding the source of pain and the condition of the tooth before recommending treatment.

02

Magnification-guided care

Using magnification and attention to detail to support precise clinical treatment.

03

Controlled working environment

Using appropriate isolation methods, including rubber dam isolation whenever indicated.

04

Tooth preservation

Focusing on preserving your natural tooth whenever a predictable and suitable outcome is possible.

What can you expect?

  • Detailed assessment before treatment
  • Explanation of the recommended procedure
  • Careful isolation and infection control
  • Attention to comfort throughout treatment
  • Guidance about the final restoration
  • Advice for maintaining your treated tooth

After root canal treatment

Your tooth may require a suitable permanent restoration depending on its remaining structure and functional demands. Follow the instructions provided after treatment and contact the clinic if you experience unexpected or persistent symptoms.
