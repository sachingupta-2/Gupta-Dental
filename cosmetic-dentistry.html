COMMON QUESTIONS

Cosmetic dentistry, explained.

Cosmetic treatment should be planned to complement your natural smile. The aim is to create an appearance that is harmonious with your teeth, facial features and overall appearance.

Suitability depends on your existing dental condition, oral health and treatment goals. A consultation and examination are important before deciding which options are appropriate.

Some treatments can address both appearance and function. Treatment planning should consider your overall oral health rather than aesthetics alone.
