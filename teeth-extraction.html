TEETH EXTRACTION

Removing a tooth when it is no longer the best option.

Tooth extraction is sometimes recommended when a tooth cannot be predictably restored or is causing pain, infection or other dental problems. Our approach focuses on careful assessment, appropriate planning and comfortable treatment.

A CAREFUL DECISION

Extraction is not always the first choice.

Whenever possible, the goal is to preserve a natural tooth. However, there are situations where removing a tooth may be the most appropriate treatment for protecting your oral health.

01

Assess

We first examine the tooth and surrounding tissues to understand the condition and determine whether the tooth can be predictably treated.

WHEN EXTRACTION MAY BE REQUIRED

When removing a tooth may be appropriate.

A tooth may sometimes require extraction when restorative treatment is no longer predictable or when the tooth is affecting surrounding oral tissues.

  • ✓ A tooth is severely damaged or extensively decayed.
  • ✓ The tooth cannot be predictably restored.
  • ✓ Infection or significant dental pain is associated with the tooth.
  • ✓ A problematic wisdom tooth requires removal.
  • ✓ Removal is recommended as part of a broader treatment plan.

PRESERVING NATURAL TEETH

The first question is always: Can the tooth be saved?

At Dr. Gupta's Dental Clinic, preservation of natural teeth is an important part of treatment planning. Extraction is considered when a tooth cannot be predictably saved or when removing it is the more appropriate option for long-term oral health.

Where suitable, alternatives such as restorative treatment or root canal therapy may be considered before deciding on extraction.
