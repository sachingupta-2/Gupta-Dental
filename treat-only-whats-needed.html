Our Difference

Treat Only What's Needed

No commissions, no upselling. If a simpler, more conservative option solves your problem, that's the one Dr. Gupta will recommend — every time.

← Back to Our Difference

Why this matters more than it sounds

A lot of dental over-treatment isn't dishonest in an obvious way — it's a filling recommended where a watch-and-wait would do, or a crown suggested where a simpler restoration holds up just as well. Dr. Gupta's rule is straightforward: recommend the least invasive option that genuinely solves the problem, and only escalate when the clinical picture actually calls for it.

What conservative treatment planning looks like

A treatment-driven approach

  • Recommendation tied to revenue targets
  • Bigger procedure offered by default
  • Options rarely explained in full

Dr. Gupta's approach

  • Recommendation tied to clinical need only
  • Simplest effective option offered first
  • Every option walked through before you decide
  • 1
    No commission-based referrals Dr. Gupta doesn't earn anything from steering you toward a particular lab, material, or add-on procedure.
  • 2
    Options explained before a plan is set You hear the conservative option and the more involved one, along with the trade-offs, before any decision is made.
  • 3
    Natural tooth structure preserved wherever possible Treatment is planned to keep as much of your original tooth intact as it safely can, rather than defaulting to replacement.
  • 4
    Second opinions are welcomed, not discouraged If you want to verify a recommendation elsewhere before proceeding, that's never treated as a problem here.
