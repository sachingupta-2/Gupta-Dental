Why patients notice the difference

Magnification-guided, composite-first dentistry

Most fillings are done by feel. Dr. Gupta works under high magnification for every restorative procedure — spotting decay margins and shaping composite fillings with a precision that's simply not visible to the naked eye.

Combined with a strict "treat only what's needed" philosophy, it means fewer redos, longer-lasting fillings, and no procedures you didn't actually need.

Dr. Tanupriya Gupta

MDS · Principal Dentist · 20 Years of Experience

Dr. Tanupriya Gupta, MDS, founded Dr. Gupta's Dental Clinic in 2013 with a simple standard: treat every patient the way she'd want her own family treated. That means unhurried appointments, magnification-guided restorative work, and an unwavering rule against unnecessary treatment.

Before starting her own practice, she spent over five years at AIIMS, New Delhi, trained at PGI Chandigarh, and served as faculty at the Faculty of Dental Sciences, Jamia Millia Islamia. She completed her MDS at Manipal and her BDS at Nair Hospital, Mumbai.

She's known among patients for being soft-spoken, thorough, and scrupulously careful — walking every patient through their options before recommending the simplest one that actually solves the problem.
