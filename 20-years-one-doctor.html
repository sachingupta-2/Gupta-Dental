Why continuity of care actually matters

Many clinics rotate patients between whichever dentist is free that day. That works fine for a single check-up, but it breaks down over years — a new dentist each visit means re-explaining your history, re-reading X-rays cold, and losing the small details a doctor only picks up by treating you repeatedly. At Dr. Gupta's Dental Clinic, that never happens. Every visit, for twenty years, has been with the same doctor.
