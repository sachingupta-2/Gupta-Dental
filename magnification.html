What are dental loupes?

Dental loupes are specially designed magnifying glasses worn by dentists during treatment. Our loupes provide 7.5× magnification, meaning the treatment area appears around seven and a half times larger, allowing us to see structures in much greater detail.

⌕

Think of it like the zoom on your phone camera.

When you zoom in, you see details you couldn't see before. Dental loupes work in a similar way, except the dentist sees the enlarged view in real time while performing the procedure.

The difference
in what we see

To the naked eye, many parts of a tooth can look smooth and simple. With 7.5× magnification, we can see finer details, such as tiny cracks, small openings and subtle changes, that may not be visible otherwise.

An everyday example

Imagine trying to thread a needle. You can see the needle easily, but the tiny hole is difficult to see without getting closer or using magnification. Dentistry often involves working with similarly small structures.

Why is it especially useful
in Root Canal Treatment?

Root canals can be very narrow, curved and sometimes difficult to locate. Magnification helps us clearly inspect the inside of the tooth, all the canals (including additional canals like MB2) and work with greater precision.
