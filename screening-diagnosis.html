A THOUGHTFUL START

Good dentistry begins with the right diagnosis.

Dental problems do not always announce themselves with pain. Some conditions can develop gradually while remaining unnoticed during everyday life.

A careful examination allows us to understand what is happening, identify areas that may require attention and determine the most appropriate course of care.

Our goal is not simply to find a problem, but to understand its cause, extent and significance before recommending treatment.

COMPREHENSIVE ASSESSMENT

Looking beyond the obvious.

A dental examination is more than checking for cavities. We consider the overall condition of your teeth, gums, bite and oral environment.

01

Teeth & Restorations

Assessment of existing teeth, fillings, crowns and other restorations to identify areas requiring attention.

02

Gum Health

Evaluation of gum health and supporting tissues to identify signs of inflammation or periodontal concerns.

03

Bite & Function

Consideration of how the teeth meet and function together as part of the overall oral assessment.

04

Early Detection

Identifying developing concerns early can help guide timely preventive care and appropriate treatment.

OUR DIAGNOSTIC PROCESS

From examination to understanding.

Every diagnosis begins with listening, examination and careful clinical judgement.

01

We begin by understanding your concerns, dental history and any symptoms you may be experiencing.

02

Clinical Examination

A detailed clinical examination helps assess the condition of your teeth, gums and surrounding oral structures.

03

Investigate When Needed

Where appropriate, additional diagnostic information may be used to understand a concern more clearly.

04

Explain & Plan

Findings are discussed with you before deciding whether treatment, monitoring or preventive care is appropriate.

CLINICAL JUDGEMENT

Not every problem needs immediate treatment.

A diagnosis is not simply a list of things that could be treated. It is an understanding of what needs attention, what can be monitored and what may benefit from prevention.

We believe treatment decisions should be based on careful assessment and individual circumstances rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.
